### Fixed

- **The jar no longer bundles Mozilla Rhino, which is MPL-2.0.** It came in with swagger-parser's
  Swagger 1.x converter, which apilytics never supported. License policy scanners flagged the
  jar as non-compliant because of it. The converter is gone, so the jar is about 5 MB smaller and
  holds only Apache-2.0 and similarly permissive code. Swagger 2.0 and OpenAPI 3.0/3.1 specs
  read as before (#350).
